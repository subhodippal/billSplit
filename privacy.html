Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

SplitEasy (“we”, “the app”) helps groups track shared expenses and work out who owes whom. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. SplitEasy is a free, non-commercial project.

1. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google

  • Your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We never see or store your Google password.

What you add to the app

  • Splits — name, purpose, currency and an optional location.
  • People — the names you add to a split. These are just labels you type; they don't need to be real names or have accounts.
  • Entries — amount, date and time, purpose, payment method, who paid, who it's shared with, an optional note and an optional location.
  • Sharing — the email addresses you invite to a split, and who has access to it.

Location (optional)

Only when you tap 📍 Locate, your browser asks for permission and shares your current coordinates. We save them with the split or entry and send them to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to look up a place name. We never track your location in the background.

Stored on your device

The app uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, remember where you were, and work offline. Nothing is used for advertising or cross-site tracking. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and show your name on the entries you add.
  • To save your splits and sync them live with the people you share them with.
  • To calculate balances and settle-up suggestions.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use it for any purpose other than running the app.

3. Who can see your information

  • People you share a split with can see that split's details, entries and the names and emails of others with access.
  • Service providers that run the app:
    • Supabase — sign-in, database and live sync.
    • Google — Google sign-in and web fonts.
    • GitHub Pages — hosts the website.
    • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — place names, only when you use Locate.

We only disclose information otherwise if required by law.

4. Security and storage

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with Supabase. Database access rules ensure you can only read and change splits you own or that were shared with you.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • You can delete any entry, or delete a split you own (this removes it for everyone it's shared with), or leave a split shared with you — all from inside the app.
  • To delete your account and everything linked to it, open a request on our GitHub Issues page (don't include private details there — we'll follow up). You can also remove SplitEasy's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Children

SplitEasy is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted in the app.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: github.com/subhodippal/billSplit/issues.
